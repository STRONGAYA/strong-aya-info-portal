Accessibility

What we want

STRONG-AYA wants everyone to be able to use this portal, including people with a disability. We aim at the European standard for accessible websites, EN 301 549, which asks for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. This statement covers the test version of the STRONG-AYA Info Portal at strongaya.github.io/strong-aya-info-portal, in English and in its translations.

How accessible is the portal now?

The portal is partially compliant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Most of it works with a keyboard, a screen reader, large text and a dark screen, but we know about these points. The portal is a research prototype with test data, and parts of it will still change.

  • The pie chart and the bar chart are drawn as pictures (SVG). A screen reader gets a one-sentence summary and the table view gives the same numbers, but the charts themselves cannot be explored with the keyboard. Reason: does not meet the standard yet.
  • The icon array (the 100 small figures) is summarised in one sentence for screen readers. Reason: may not meet the standard; not yet checked with screen reader users.
  • The articles from beatcancer.eu and the national websites are made by other organisations and open in a new tab; we cannot change how accessible they are. Reason: content of others, outside the scope of this statement.
  • Machine-translated pages are translated automatically (by DeepL) and may contain mistakes. Every translated page says so at the top and links to the English page. Reason: outside the standard; reviewers are checking the translations.
  • Read aloud uses the voices on your own device. Some devices have no voice for your language, or only a poor one, and phones stop reading when the screen locks. Reason: outside the standard; your browser's own read-aloud feature is the alternative.
  • Some buttons do nothing yet, such as "Set as default", "Compare" and some filters. They show "Coming soon" when you point at them or reach them with the keyboard. Reason: does not meet the standard yet; they will be finished or removed.
  • Not tested with assistive technology yet. Nobody who uses a screen reader, a switch or voice control has tested the pages yet; we checked them ourselves (see below).

What the portal offers

  • The accessibility bar under the purple banner: Contrast for stronger colours, Dark for a dark screen, Size for four text sizes, Audio to have the page read aloud, and Language and Country. Your choices stay on your own device.
  • Keyboard. Every button, menu and dialog works with the keyboard, the element you are on is clearly marked, and a "Skip to main content" link takes you past the header.
  • Screen readers. Every figure has a spoken summary of the numbers, and the table view shows the same numbers as text. Decorative icons are hidden, and links that open a new tab say so.
  • Plain language. We write short sentences and explain medical words in the Glossary.

About this statement

We prepared this statement on 20 October 2026. It is based on a self-assessment by the portal's developers: on 20 October 2026 we reviewed the markup, styling, keyboard use and colour contrast of the pages against WCAG 2.1 level AA by hand, without automated tools or users of assistive technology. Last reviewed on 20 October 2026. We update this statement when the portal changes, and at least once a year.

STRONG-AYA is an interdisciplinary, multi-stakeholder European network to improve healthcare services, research and outcomes for adolescents and young adults (AYAs) with cancer.
