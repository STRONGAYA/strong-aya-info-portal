Anxiety Depression Worry Mental health support

The numbers about depression are test data. They are based on the HADS questionnaire (Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale) filled in by young people with cancer.

The numbers are based on a group of about 3300 young adults who were treated in university medical centres in the Netherlands. This information was last updated on 5 August 2024.

Feeling overwhelmed?

Talk with people close to you, or meet people with similar experiences through the European Network of Youth Cancer Survivors at beatcancer.eu. Do you need professional help or advice? Contact us.

STRONG-AYA is an interdisciplinary, multi-stakeholder European network to improve healthcare services, research and outcomes for adolescents and young adults (AYAs) with cancer.
